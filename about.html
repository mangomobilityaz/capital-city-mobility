About Capital City Mobility

Capital City Mobility LLC is a Las Vegas, Nevada rental company that provides mobility scooters to visitors and residents across the Las Vegas metro area, including the Las Vegas Strip and the surrounding hotel corridor.

We serve people with limited mobility, including tourists, convention attendees, and local residents, by offering short-term and extended rentals of powered mobility equipment. Every rental includes delivery, pickup, and a basic in-person orientation on safe operation.

What we offer

Flexible plans

Daily and weekly rentals, scheduled around conventions and events.

Free delivery

Delivery and pickup to Strip hotels, resorts, and local addresses throughout the valley.

Safe and simple

Clean, charged scooters and a short walkthrough at drop-off.
