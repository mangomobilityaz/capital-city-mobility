About Capital City Mobility

A Las Vegas, Nevada rental company that helps people get around the city comfortably.

Capital City Mobility LLC rents mobility scooters to visitors and residents across the Las Vegas metro area, including the Las Vegas Strip and the surrounding hotel corridor.

We serve people with limited mobility, including tourists, convention attendees, and local residents, with short-term and extended rentals of powered mobility equipment. Every rental includes delivery, pickup, and a basic in-person orientation on safe operation.

Our goal is simple: on-time delivery, clean and reliable scooters, and friendly service, so you can enjoy your time in Las Vegas.
